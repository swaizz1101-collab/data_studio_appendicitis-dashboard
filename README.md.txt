# Appendicitis Diagnosis Dashboard Using Google Looker Studio

## Project Overview
This dashboard was developed using Google Looker Studio to analyze a medical dataset related to appendicitis diagnosis. The dataset includes patient demographics, symptoms, laboratory results, and clinical outcomes, providing a comprehensive basis for analysis. It consists of **1,500 patient records**, enabling reliable identification of patterns and trends.

The dashboard presents data through interactive visualizations, including scorecards and charts. Key performance indicators displayed at the top—such as total number of patients, average age (**25.4 years**), average white blood cell (WBC) count (**13.27**), and number of appendicitis cases—provide a quick summary of the dataset. The analysis shows that approximately **79.3%** of patients are diagnosed with appendicitis, making it the most prevalent condition.

## Key Insights & Visualizations
* **Patient Diagnosis:** A pie chart illustrates the distribution of final diagnoses, enabling comparison between appendicitis and other medical conditions.
* **Diagnosis by Gender & Severity:** Bar charts are used to analyze gender distribution, severity levels, and abdominal pain locations. 
* **Clinical Indicators:** The results indicate that right lower quadrant (RLQ) pain is the most commonly reported symptom, which is a key clinical indicator of appendicitis. Severity analysis shows that most cases are uncomplicated, with fewer cases classified as complicated.
* **Laboratory Markers:** A scatter plot is used to visualize the relationship between WBC count and C-reactive protein (CRP) levels, demonstrating that higher values are associated with increased inflammation and infection severity. These findings highlight the importance of laboratory markers in diagnosis.

## Features
Interactive filters, such as gender, diagnosis, and severity, allow users to dynamically explore the dataset, enhancing usability and analytical depth.

## Conclusion
Overall, this dashboard effectively identifies patterns in symptoms, laboratory findings, and diagnoses, supporting data-driven insights and informed decision-making in medical analysis.
